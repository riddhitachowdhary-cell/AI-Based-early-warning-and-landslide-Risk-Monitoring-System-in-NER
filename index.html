ABOUT LANDSAFE CONNECT

Technology for Safer Communities

LandSafe Connect is an AI-powered disaster risk monitoring platform designed to support communities living in vulnerable regions.

EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS

Know What To Do During a Landslide

Follow official emergency instructions and use LandSafe Connect as a decision-support information platform.

Stay away from unstable slopes, cracks and rapidly changing terrain. Move to a safer location when instructed by authorities.

Avoid walking or driving through flood water. Move to higher ground when instructed by emergency authorities.

Follow official warnings, keep emergency contacts accessible and move to designated safe areas when necessary.

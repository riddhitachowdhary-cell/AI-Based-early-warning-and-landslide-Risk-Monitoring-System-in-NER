Demo Information

The probability values shown on this page are demonstration values for the LandSafe Connect interface. They are not live disaster predictions. Once the backend, environmental data sources, and trained machine-learning models are connected, these values can be generated from actual input data.

LandSafe Connect provides AI-based risk assessment and emergency information for decision support. It does not replace official disaster-management warnings or emergency services.

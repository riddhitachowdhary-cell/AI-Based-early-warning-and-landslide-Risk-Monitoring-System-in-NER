Flood Safety

Important actions before, during and after flooding.

  • Monitor official weather and disaster warnings.
  • Keep important documents in a waterproof container.
  • Keep drinking water and essential food supplies ready.
  • Charge your phone and power bank.
  • Know the evacuation route recommended by authorities.
  • Move important electrical appliances and valuables to higher locations if flooding is expected.

  • Follow evacuation instructions from authorities.
  • Move to higher ground when necessary.
  • Never walk or drive through rapidly moving floodwater.
  • Stay away from electrical equipment and exposed wires.
  • Keep emergency supplies and communication devices with you.
  • Stay informed through official alerts and trusted sources.

  • Return home only when authorities say it is safe.
  • Avoid contaminated or stagnant water.
  • Avoid damaged electrical systems.
  • Report dangerous infrastructure or hazards.
  • Check family members and neighbors who may need assistance.
Landslide Safety

Recognize warning signs and respond quickly.

  • New cracks appearing on roads, walls or ground.
  • Unusual movement or tilting of trees, poles or structures.
  • Sudden changes in water flow or drainage.
  • Small rockfalls or soil movement on slopes.
  • Unusual sounds such as cracking or rumbling from a slope.

  • Move away from the path of moving soil, rocks or debris.
  • Move toward safer and more stable ground when possible.
  • Follow evacuation instructions from authorities.
  • Stay away from unstable slopes and damaged roads.
  • Warn others nearby if it can be done safely.

  • Do not return to an affected area until it is declared safe.
  • Be alert for additional landslides.
  • Avoid damaged roads, bridges and structures.
  • Report new hazards to local authorities.
Emergency Kit

Keep essential emergency supplies ready.

Communication & Power
  • Fully charged mobile phone
  • Power bank
  • Torch or flashlight
  • Extra batteries
Food & Water
  • Drinking water
  • Ready-to-eat food
  • Basic utensils
  • Water purification supplies if appropriate
Important Documents
  • Identification documents
  • Emergency contact information
  • Important documents stored safely
Basic Supplies
  • First-aid supplies
  • Essential medicines
  • Basic hygiene supplies
  • Extra clothing
Evacuation Checklist

Things to remember when leaving an unsafe area.

Quick Safety Rules
DO
  • Stay informed through trusted official sources.
  • Move to safer areas when instructed.
  • Keep emergency supplies ready.
  • Help others when it is safe to do so.
DON'T
  • Do not ignore official evacuation warnings.
  • Do not enter rapidly moving floodwater.
  • Do not approach unstable slopes.
  • Do not return to an affected area until it is safe.
